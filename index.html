ABOUT FARM2WOOL

One journey.
Complete transparency.

Farm2Wool creates a digital connection between the farmer, manufacturer and customer. Every wool batch can carry its own quality information, traceability identity and transaction journey.

01
🌾

Farmer

Farmers register their wool batches with location, date, quantity, breed and all 16 required wool quality parameters.

02
🏭

03
👤

Customers can discover the origin, quality and complete Farm2Wool journey of registered wool and finished products.

WOOL QUALITY SYSTEM

16 parameters.
One complete wool profile.

Farm2Wool records detailed wool characteristics before purchase and allows the manufacturer to independently verify the same parameters after inspection.

01

Fibre Thickness

Fibre diameter or micron measurement recorded for the wool batch.

02

Fibre Length

Records the fibre length of the wool supplied by the farmer.

03

Moisture Content

Records the moisture level of the registered wool batch.

04

Cleanliness

Indicates the cleanliness condition of the wool.

05

Grease / Lanolin

Records the grease or lanolin content present in the wool.

06

Wool Yield

Records the usable wool yield from the registered batch.

07

Fibre Strength

Records the measured strength of the wool fibres.

08

Colour

Records the observed colour and appearance of the wool.

09

Fibre Uniformity

Records how consistent the fibres are throughout the wool batch.

10

Crimp

Records the natural waviness or crimp characteristics of the wool.

11

Contamination

Records unwanted materials or foreign matter present in the wool.

12

Odour / Storage

Records odour and the storage condition of the wool.

13

Pest / Damage

Records pest activity, physical damage or deterioration.

14

Breed / Wool Type

Identifies the sheep breed and type of wool being registered.

15

Shearing Condition

Records the condition and quality of the wool after shearing.

16

Lot Consistency

Indicates how consistent the wool is across the entire registered lot.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Everything connected.

A complete digital workflow for transparent wool trading and traceability.

🌾

Wool Batch Tracking

Every farmer can register a unique wool batch with its origin, quantity, breed, date and detailed quality information.

◉

16-Parameter Quality

Capture a complete wool profile and compare farmer-submitted values with manufacturer inspection results.

₹

Indicative Market Price

The platform can calculate an indicative wool price based on breed, quantity and recorded quality information.

🛒

Wool Marketplace

Manufacturers can browse registered farmer batches and purchase a specific batch through the platform.

🔒

Payment Hold

The purchase amount remains on hold until the manufacturer's inspection and quality verification is completed.

🔍

Manufacturer Verification

Manufacturers enter their own verified values after physically inspecting the purchased wool.

⚖

Quality Comparison

Farmer-submitted and manufacturer-verified values remain separate so the system can compare the two records.

▣

QR Traceability

Each wool batch receives a generated QR containing its batch details and all 16 quality parameters, with manufacturer verification and market data added after inspection.

QUALITY & PAYMENT WORKFLOW

A transparent transaction
from purchase to verification.

Farm2Wool separates the farmer's original quality declaration from the manufacturer's inspection, creating a transparent verification process.

01
📝

Register

Farmer enters the batch information and all 16 wool quality parameters.

02
🛒

Purchase

Manufacturer views available batches and purchases a selected batch.

03
🔒

Payment Held

The purchase amount remains held while the physical wool is inspected.

04
🔍

Inspect

Manufacturer enters independently verified values for the same 16 parameters.

05
⚖

Compare

The farmer's original declaration is compared against the manufacturer's inspection.

06
✓

Resolve

Matching quality releases payment. A failed inspection triggers the return and refund workflow.
